Nondominium group model: requirements for Moss and standalone

Status: ○ proposed · ✓ accepted · ✎ reworded · ✕ rejected. Click the current one again to go back to proposed; keys p, a, w, x on the selected row.

What a group is in standalone and in Moss, what v0.1.0 ships, and what v0.2 adds: work contexts in the group DHT, many-to-many with groups, and the federation layer. These rows are the ones proposed in the docs PR for issue #167 (documentation/requirements/lobby-dna.md and requirements.md). Set each priority and status, answer the nine questions at the bottom, and the PR is edited to match before it merges. A question whose answer changes a row is named on the row.

Decided on 2026-11-03 (taken as given here). Work contexts live in the group DHT, not in a DNA of their own (this reverses the 27 Oct lean). The Group DNA stays in both modes. In Moss the Moss group is the federation layer and there is one Nondominium group per Moss group, the tool instance's group cell, joined automatically with no invite code. v0.1.0 ships Moss only and no work context. Work contexts and groups are many-to-many (Catmaster: Sensorica, Ludociel, Sainte-Justine). Projects are not NDOs. Three sovereignty levels (NDO, work context, federation) each keep their own governance scope. The internal term is "work context", the displayed term is "project".

Open questions

Q1 Is the Lobby DNA role still present in the Moss build?

In Moss, groups are not registered in the Lobby and nobody enters an invite code (REQ-GROUP-22), so the Lobby group registry has no caller. The role is provisioned in the current happ manifest.

Q2 Where does a shared work context's canonical state live?

A work context lives in a group DHT (decided) and may be worked in by several groups (REQ-WC-03). Those two facts need a rule for which DHT holds the state when Sensorica, Ludociel and Sainte-Justine share Catmaster.

Q3 Does work-context governance cover benefit redistribution (the contribution tables)?

The 27 Oct brainstorm places redistribution at the work-context level. REQ-GROUP-17 and section 8.2 say group governance does not govern benefit distribution, which belongs to NDO governance (smart agreements).

Q4 Do the work-context entry types ship dormant in v0.1.0?

Integrity code is part of the DNA hash, so adding work-context entry types to the Group DNA in v0.2 produces a different group DNA and a new network. v0.1.0 group cells would not carry over without a migration.

Q5 Is there a federation layer in standalone mode in v0.2?

In Moss the Moss group is the federation layer. The 27 Oct brainstorm said that outside Moss the same structure could be cloned for the federation, with differently typed governance, or be a different entity. REQ-GROUP-03 keeps groups flat.

Q6 Where does federation governance live in Moss?

Moss groups carry no governance of their own. The brainstorm floated offering Moss a federation and work-context governance layer once the work exists, but did not settle it.

Q7 Where does project management live: in the work context or at the federation?

The question that started the brainstorm. It sits between the two upper levels and was left open on 27 Oct.

Q8 Does a work context ever have an NDO twin, given REQ-AGENT-02?

REQ-AGENT-02 says collective agents, projects included, have an agent face and may have an NDO as an optional digital twin. REQ-WC-02 says a work context is not an NDO.

Q9 Who may enter a work context, and is the display term aligned with Moss?

Bob Haugen's work context is open: agents arrive and leave without a contract. Group membership is invite-only (REQ-GROUP-05). The 27 Oct brainstorm also left open aligning the display term with Moss's own vocabulary to avoid clashes in the interface.
